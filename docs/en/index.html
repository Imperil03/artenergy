Before you visit

Can I bring children?

Yes. Children must be supervised by adults at all times.

Can we play our own music?

Yes, you can use your own playlist after agreeing it with the administrator.

Where can we eat and drink?

Food and drinks are allowed in designated areas.

Can we reserve the entire venue for our group?

Private use of the venue and its cost are discussed separately. A regular visit does not mean the whole venue will be closed to other guests.

What rules apply at the pools?

Jumping and diving into the pools are prohibited.

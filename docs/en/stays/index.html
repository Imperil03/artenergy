Stays in Punta Cana — bungalows and apartments

Stay at ArtEnergy for a night or a few days. Our eco-bungalows and apartments sit among palm trees and tropical greenery. Choose an open-air bungalow for a stay closer to nature or a more familiar stay in a private indoor space.

Eco bungalow

The eco-bungalow suits those who want to spend time closer to nature. It is an open structure without conventional windows, with a sleeping area and curtains. Around it are ArtEnergy’s tropical garden, palm trees and paths leading to the leisure areas.

Price — 150 USD per 24 hours.

Choose an eco-bungalow

Apartments

The apartments suit those who prefer a separate, enclosed indoor space. They are on the ArtEnergy grounds, near the pools and leisure areas.

Browse the interior photos to choose the accommodation that suits you.

Price — on request.

Ask about apartments

Enjoy the ArtEnergy grounds

During your stay, you will be surrounded by a tropical garden, near the pools, covered leisure areas and other ArtEnergy spaces.

Two pools and a tropical garden

The grounds have two pools surrounded by palm trees and greenery. Nearby are paths, covered pavilions and places to relax. Pool access during your stay is arranged separately.

Kitchen and bar

At ArtEnergy, you can order grilled skewers, home-style dishes, salads, fresh juices and cocktails. Meals during your stay are arranged in advance and charged separately.

Banya and hammam

If you would like to add a bathhouse session to your stay, you can book the banya or hammam separately. Each service has its own price and session duration.

Steam bath or hammam — which should you choose?

ArtEnergy offers two types of steam room. The steam bath has wooden benches and a stove. It suits those who prefer a traditional bathhouse experience and plan to spend a few hours in the steam room. The minimum visit is three hours.

The hammam has high humidity. It is an option for those who prefer humid steam. Hammam visits are charged by the hour, so you can arrange the duration separately.

Steam bath and hammam at ArtEnergy

The steam bath and hammam are at ArtEnergy in Bávaro, Punta Cana. The grounds also have two pools, lounge areas, a kitchen and bar.

If you would like to combine your steam room visit with time by the pool, mention this when booking. Access to the pools and other areas is arranged separately. Food, drinks and massages can be ordered at an additional cost.

Before your visit

How do I arrange a visit?

Send us your date, guest count and preferred duration. The administrator will confirm availability and arrangements.

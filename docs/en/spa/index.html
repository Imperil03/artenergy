Banya and hammam in Punta Cana

At ArtEnergy in Bávaro, you can book a banya with a wooden steam room or a hammam with humid steam. Choose the format that suits you and take your time — as a couple, with friends or with family.

Steam bath

The ArtEnergy banya has wooden benches and a stove. Spend a few hours in a traditional steam room, alternating time inside with breaks to relax.

The minimum banya booking is three hours. This is a convenient option for those who enjoy a full steam-room session and do not want to limit themselves to a short visit.

6 000 DOP for 3 hours

The hourly rate is 2 000 DOP. The minimum booking duration is 3 hours.

Book the banya

Hammam

The hammam has a different atmosphere: warm, humid air and steam filling the room. This format suits those who prefer humid heat and want to enjoy a relaxed pace.

Visits are charged by the hour. You can arrange the duration in advance to suit your plans.

2 000 DOP per hour

Book the hammam

Add more to your time at ArtEnergy

Combine a banya or hammam visit with other ways to relax on the ArtEnergy grounds. There are pools, covered leisure areas, a kitchen and a bar.

Pools and leisure areas

The grounds have two pools surrounded by palm trees and tropical greenery. If you would like to spend time by the water after the steam room, arrange the terms of your visit in advance.

Explore the venue

Kitchen and bar

During your visit, you can order home-style dishes, grilled skewers, salads, fresh juices and cocktails. Food and drinks are charged separately.

View the menu

Massage

If you would like to add a massage to your visit, mention it when booking. Availability and prices are confirmed separately.

How to book the banya or hammam

Message us on WhatsApp with your preferred date, number of guests and time. We will check availability and agree on the duration and price.

SPA and massage in Punta Cana

At ArtEnergy in Bávaro, you can book a massage, body peeling or a steam treatment. Choose an individual treatment or combine several services with a visit to the banya and hammam. We will agree your programme, duration and visit time in advance.

SPA treatments and prices

TreatmentDescriptionPrice

The duration and arrangements for each treatment are agreed when booking. The banya, hammam and additional services are charged separately.

Massage

During a massage, the practitioner works on the muscles and soft tissues of the body. Before the session, you can discuss which areas need more attention and the level of pressure you prefer.

You can plan a massage on its own or add it to a visit to the banya and hammam. Available techniques and the duration of the session are agreed when booking.

Book a massage

Body peeling

Peeling helps remove dead skin cells from the surface of the skin. During the treatment, an exfoliating product is applied to the skin and then removed.

You can include this care in a SPA programme alongside a massage or hammam visit. The type of peeling, the product and the areas treated are chosen with your skin’s characteristics in mind.

Book body peeling

Steam treatment

A steam treatment is a separate bathing procedure in which the body is warmed in a steam room using steam. Unlike an ordinary independent sauna visit, it includes an additional warming procedure.

You can book a steam treatment together with the banya. The duration, how the procedure is carried out and any items needed are agreed before your visit.

Book a steam treatment

Add time to relax at ArtEnergy to your SPA visit

ArtEnergy has a banya with a wooden steam room, a hammam, two pools and covered leisure areas. You can plan SPA treatments alongside other services at the complex.

Banya and hammam

Choose a traditional steam room with wooden benches or a hammam with humid steam. Visits are booked separately, and SPA treatments can be arranged for the same date.

Pools and leisure areas

After your treatments, you can spend time on the ArtEnergy grounds, relax by the pool or order food and drinks from the menu. Access to the pools and other areas is agreed when booking.

Massage and SPA treatments in Punta Cana

At ArtEnergy in Bávaro, you can book a massage, body peeling or a steam treatment. Treatments are available by prior arrangement. You can combine them with a visit to the banya or hammam.

Massage

You can plan a massage during your time at ArtEnergy. When you book, we will explain which types of massage are available, how long a session lasts and how much it costs.

If you also plan to visit the banya or hammam, you can arrange all the services for the same date.

Ask about massage

Body peeling

Body peeling is a skin care treatment intended to cleanse the skin. At ArtEnergy, you can include it in your relaxation plans alongside other SPA services.

The types of peeling available, the products used and the duration of the treatment are confirmed when booking.

Ask about body peeling

Steam treatment

A steam treatment is a separate bathing procedure involving warmth and steam. You can book it as an additional service when visiting the sauna.

The duration and how the procedure is carried out are agreed in advance.

Ask about a steam treatment

SPA treatments and time in the banya

ArtEnergy also has a banya with a wooden steam room and a hammam with humid steam.

If you would like to combine several services, you can arrange them for the same date. Banya visits, hammam visits and SPA treatments are charged separately.

Explore the banya and hammam

How to book a SPA treatment

Message us on WhatsApp with the treatment you are interested in, your preferred date and time. We will confirm availability, duration and price.

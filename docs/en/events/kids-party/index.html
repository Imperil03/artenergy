Children’s parties in Punta Cana

Celebrate your child’s birthday or hold a family celebration at ArtEnergy. The grounds have pools, covered leisure areas, a kitchen and a bar with a children’s menu. Bring children and adults together, enjoy a celebration lunch and spend time outdoors.

How to hold a children’s party at ArtEnergy

Gather friends and family, prepare birthday wishes, games and a party programme. At ArtEnergy, you can spend time by the pools, arrange food for your guests and relax in covered areas among the palm trees.

If you plan to invite a children’s entertainer, decorate the venue or hold a themed party, tell us in advance. We will discuss your ideas and what can be arranged for the celebration.

Children must be supervised by adults at all times.

Children’s menu and celebration cake

ArtEnergy has a separate menu for younger guests: hamburgers, cheeseburgers, chicken nuggets, fries and juices. Adults can choose dishes from the main kitchen and bar menu.

You can order a birthday cake in advance — honey cake, Napoleon cake or Bird’s milk cake. Cake decoration is charged separately. If you have any decoration requests, please mention them when ordering.

View the menu

Children’s party conditions and prices

Regular visits to ArtEnergy require a minimum spend on food and drinks. During the day, it is 500 DOP per person for a six-hour period; in the evening, it is 1 000 DOP. The full amount goes towards orders from the menu.

Visiting conditions for children are confirmed separately.

If you are planning a celebration exclusively for invited guests and would like to use a separate area or the entire venue, tell us in advance. The availability and price of this arrangement are discussed individually.

More about visiting conditions

Would you like to stay overnight?

ArtEnergy has eco-bungalows and apartments on the grounds. If you would like to stay with your family after the party or accommodate visiting guests, you can book accommodation separately.

Wedding in Punta Cana

Celebrate your wedding at ArtEnergy — among palm trees, beside pools and covered lounge areas. Gather with loved ones for a wedding dinner or have a party with your own programme. The event format and arrangements for using the venue are agreed in advance.

Photos of the venue

How to celebrate your wedding at ArtEnergy

ArtEnergy can host an intimate celebration with loved ones or a larger gathering of friends and relatives. The grounds have two pools, a tropical garden and covered lounge areas where guests can spend time together.

During the day, you can have a photo session among the palm trees; in the evening, gather for a wedding dinner with congratulations and music.

If you are planning a wedding ceremony on the grounds, decorations, an outside host, a photographer or musical entertainment, tell us your plans in advance. We will discuss what the venue can accommodate and the arrangements for your event.

Wedding dinner and celebration cake

ArtEnergy has a kitchen and bar. For your wedding evening, choose food and drinks from the menu: meat dishes, salads, appetisers, fresh juices and cocktails.

A wedding cake can be ordered in advance. Its size, decoration and price are calculated individually.

If you are planning a particular order of service or a separate menu for guests, tell us your preferences when discussing the celebration.

View the kitchen and bar menu

Wedding arrangements and cost

Arrangements depend on the chosen format, number of guests and event duration.

Regular visits to ArtEnergy require a minimum order of food and drinks. The full amount goes towards your order from the menu.

If you would like a separate wedding event, a particular area or the entire venue exclusively for your guests, the availability of that format and its price are agreed individually.

Decorations, outside professionals, music and other details of the celebration are also discussed in advance.

ArtEnergy visiting arrangements

Accommodation for the newlyweds and guests

After the wedding celebration, you can stay on the ArtEnergy grounds. Eco-bungalows and apartments are available for accommodation.

If you are planning accommodation for relatives or friends, tell us the number of guests and your dates — we will confirm the available options and booking arrangements.

A party with friends in Punta Cana

Get together with friends at ArtEnergy — a tropical venue with pools, covered lounge areas and a bar. Spend the evening over dinner and cocktails, catch up or hold a party with your own programme.

An evening with friends at ArtEnergy

A gathering with friends does not need a celebration programme. Meet for dinner, order drinks and spend the evening by the pool.

If you are planning dancing, your own playlist or a DJ performance, agree the music programme and arrangements in advance.

Food and drinks for your group

ArtEnergy has a kitchen and bar. Order grilled skewers, salads, starters and other dishes from the menu for your gathering. The bar offers cocktails, wine, spirits and non-alcoholic drinks.

For a large group, it is best to choose the main dishes and discuss the number of portions in advance. This lets you arrange dining before the party begins.

View the food and bar menu

Party arrangements and prices

Regular visits to ArtEnergy require a minimum spend on food and drinks. The following arrangements apply in the evening and at night:

  • 18:00–00:00 — 1 000 DOP per person.
  • 00:00–06:00 — 1 000 DOP per person.

The full minimum spend goes toward food and drinks from the menu. Each time period is paid separately.

If you are planning a private party just for your guests, discuss individual arrangements for using a particular area or the entire ArtEnergy grounds.

The possibility of holding your party at the chosen time, music and additional services are agreed in advance.

All visiting rates

Would you like to stay overnight?

The ArtEnergy grounds have eco-bungalows and apartments. If you would prefer not to leave after the party, accommodation can be booked separately.

An overnight visit to the venue does not include accommodation. Availability and booking arrangements are checked in advance.

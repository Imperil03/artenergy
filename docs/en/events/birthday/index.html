Birthday celebrations in Punta Cana

Celebrate your birthday at ArtEnergy — a venue with pools, covered leisure areas, a kitchen and a bar. Gather with friends and family, spend time by the water and plan your own celebration.

Plan your birthday celebration

Photos of the venue

How to celebrate your birthday at ArtEnergy

Come to ArtEnergy with a small group or invite more guests. The grounds have two pools, places to relax among the palm trees and covered areas with sofas.

Spend the day by the pool, enjoy a birthday lunch or dinner, or prepare your own programme with birthday wishes and music. If you are planning decorations, outside professionals or other elements of the celebration, tell us your ideas in advance — we will discuss what the venue can accommodate.

Food, drinks and birthday cake

ArtEnergy has a kitchen and a bar. The menu includes grilled skewers, home-style dishes, salads, snacks, fresh juices and cocktails. Choose dishes for your group and order drinks during the celebration.

You can order a birthday cake in advance: honey cake, Napoleon cake or Bird’s milk cake. Cake decoration is priced separately.

View the menu

Conditions and prices

The price depends on the type of celebration you choose.

Regular visits require a minimum spend on food and drinks. During the day, it is 500 DOP per person for a six-hour period; in the evening and at night, it is 1 000 DOP. The full amount goes towards orders from the menu.

If you would like to hold a separate event or use the entire venue exclusively for your group, conditions and prices are discussed individually.

More about visiting conditions

Would you like to stay overnight?

ArtEnergy has eco-bungalows and apartments on the grounds. If you plan to stay overnight after the celebration, you can book accommodation separately.
